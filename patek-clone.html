A Long-Term Vision

Innovation and
Tradition

We have been pursuing traditional Genevan watchmaking artistry without interruption since 1839, safeguarding rare handcrafts such as enameling and guillochage that would have otherwise disappeared. At the same time, we nurture a tradition of innovation, relying on the latest technologies to further push the boundaries of watchmaking.
